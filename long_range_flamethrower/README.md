# Red Long-Range Flamethrower Turret

Adds a red variant of the vanilla flamethrower turret.

- Maximum range: 60 tiles (vanilla: 30)
- Firing arc: 360 degrees
- Minimum range: 6 tiles (unchanged)
- Fluid consumption: 0.2 (unchanged)
- Recipe: 1 standard flamethrower turret and 2 engine units
- Technology: unlocked by Flamethrower technology

The mod reuses the vanilla graphics and tints their mask layers red, so it does
not duplicate Factorio's image assets.

All mod-specific prototype names, balance values, appearance values, recipe
ingredients, crafting time, output, and source-prototype selections are kept in
`config.lua`.
